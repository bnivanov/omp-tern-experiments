# Handoff

The effective writer updates this file in place. `Writer released: yes` releases that coordinator and its own unfinished claims, not assignments of workers that may still be live. Preserve those assignments, checkouts, and last reports under Claims. An authorized successor appends a writer-transfer Revision, updates the writer/runtime provenance, sets release to `no`, and retags only released coordinator claims. A punctuated runtime id is provenance, not the alphanumeric workflow Session. Do not take over an unreleased writer. Card status is not acceptance evidence. Leave tables empty until a real result exists.

Optional boundary labels may stay blank. They help a later session find the checkout and records. They do not add a writer, a board, or a required bundle.
